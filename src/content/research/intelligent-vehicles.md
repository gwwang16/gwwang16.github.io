My vehicle research connects decision-making, motion planning, and control, with particular attention to mountainous roads and constrained parking environments.

## Planning in complex environments

Recent work includes terrain-aware off-road path planning using Multi-terrain RRT*, and parking trajectory planning through joint spatial and temporal search. These studies consider terrain traversability or moving obstacles alongside the geometry of a feasible vehicle path. A related line of work studies real-time LiDAR point-cloud semantic segmentation for mobile platforms.

## Learning-based control and energy management

We study adaptive deep reinforcement learning for autonomous driving on mountain roads. For fuel-cell hybrid buses, our work incorporates terrain characteristics into energy management and altitude variation into air-supply control and net-power optimization.

## Current projects

Current funded work focuses on minimally invasive safety arbitration under time-varying constraints and omnidirectional active collision avoidance for autonomous buses. These projects connect planning and control with the physical constraints that govern vehicle safety.
